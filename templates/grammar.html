IELTS Academic Grammar

High-Impact Grammar Structures for Band 7.0+

Grammatical Range & Accuracy accounts for 25% of your total Writing and Speaking score. Master these core academic structures to avoid repetitive simple sentences.

Unit 01

Complex Sentences with Concession & Contrast

Writing Task 2 Essential

Instead of writing two short sentences with "but" or "however", use concessive clauses (While, Whereas, Although, Despite the fact that) to show mature sentence control.

Band 5.5 - 6.0 Structure

"Some people like electric cars. But they are still very expensive for normal consumers."

Band 7.5+ Academic Transformation

"While electric vehicles offer undeniable ecological benefits, their prohibitive upfront costs remain a deterrent for average consumers."

Unit 02

Inversion Structures (Not only... but also, Rarely, Seldom)

High-Scorer Differentiator

Inversion inverts the auxiliary verb and subject when starting a sentence with negative or restrictive adverbials. Used sparingly (once per essay), it signals advanced syntactical mastery.

Formula: Not only + Auxiliary + Subject + Verb, but Subject also + Verb

"Not only does international tourism stimulate local commerce, but it also fosters invaluable cross-cultural understanding."

Unit 03

Participle Clauses & Active/Passive Reduction

Task 1 & 2 Fluency

Participle clauses allow you to pack more relevant detail into a single concise sentence without overloading on relative pronouns (which, that, who).

Standard Relative Clause

"The proportion of households which relied on solar power rose by 14%."

Participle Clause

"The proportion of households relying on solar power surged by 14%."
